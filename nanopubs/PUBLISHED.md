# Published nanopub chain — URI registry

This file is the canonical registry of published nanopub URIs for this chain: *Do the most socially vulnerable residents at pluvial flood risk in Logroño have less room to green near their homes than the least vulnerable?* (question-rooted, built on the [Logroño flood-adaptation scenarios analysis v0.2.0](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23302113); outcome repository: release v0.1.0, [10.5281/zenodo.23302695](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23302695)). It extends the [city-wide greening chain](https://github.com/annefou/logrono-greening-flood-risk-vulnerability) (Outcome <https://w3id.org/sciencelive/np/RA7f_o-KNPr1Rq2OPPJD47XcUYtPvIRF4rRDqFg-tsZgA>).

**Finding (AIDA):** *In Logroño, the most socially vulnerable residents at pluvial flood risk have less land that could be greened near their homes than the least vulnerable.* Outcome: validated, high confidence.

View a step in the Science Live viewer: `https://platform.sciencelive4all.org/np/?uri=<URI>`.

## Format

URIs from Science Live are of the form `https://w3id.org/sciencelive/np/RA…`. URIs from Nanodash (used as a fallback when the Science Live UI hits a bug) are of the form `https://w3id.org/np/RA…`. Both are valid and citable.

If a URI is not in the Science Live namespace, view it via the Science Live viewer by wrapping the URI:

```
https://platform.sciencelive4all.org/np/?uri=<full-URI>
```
