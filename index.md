# Room to green near the homes of socially vulnerable residents at pluvial flood risk in Logroño

> **Do the most socially vulnerable residents at pluvial flood risk in Logroño (Spain) have less room to green near their homes than the least vulnerable?** — one FORRT nanopublication chain, built on the [Logroño flood-adaptation scenarios analysis v0.2.0](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23302113).

**Finding:** *In Logroño, the most socially vulnerable residents at pluvial flood risk have less land that could be greened near their homes than the least vulnerable.*

![Headline figure](figures/main_result.png)

The notebooks: (1) the analysis release, Cadastre buildings and Urban Atlas, (2) land that could be greened per building, (3) the finding by vulnerability group, (4) the figure. The chain is listed in [`nanopubs/PUBLISHED.md`](nanopubs/PUBLISHED.md) once published.
